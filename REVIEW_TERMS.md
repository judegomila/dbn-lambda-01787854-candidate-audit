# Review terms

This repository is published openly to invite mathematical and
reproducibility review.  It is a computer-assisted proof that has not
yet been peer reviewed; see `README.md` for the status statement and
`OPEN_REVIEW_QUESTIONS.md` for the decisions being asked of referees.

Repository-original material is licensed as recorded in `LICENSE`
(MIT, source code) and `LICENSE-DOCS` (CC BY 4.0, documentation and
data), with the exceptions listed there: the authored manuscripts and
the `independent/` programs are all-rights-reserved pending author
agreements.

Third-party material remains under its own terms.  In particular, the
vendored Mosaic Intelligence material is attributed in `THIRD_PARTY.md`
and is recorded by Zenodo as CC BY 4.0, and the LGPL sources under
`vendor/` carry their own license text.

Citing this repository: see `CITATION.md`.  Please do not describe the
bound as established or peer-reviewed until independent review exists.

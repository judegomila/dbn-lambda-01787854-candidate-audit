# Conjugate-Mollified Two-Ladder Center

Let

\[
f_t=F_B+\gamma C_\kappa,
\qquad
M=M_\lambda(s_*),
\]

with real mollifier coefficients. Put

\[
MF_B=1+R_B,
\qquad
\overline M C_0=1+R_A.
\]

Since \(|M|=|\overline M|\),

\[
Mf_t
=1+R_B+\gamma\frac{M}{\overline M}(1+R_A)
 +M\gamma(C_\kappa-C_0)
\]

whenever \(M\ne0\). The same identity extends across a hypothetical
\(M=0\) after clearing \(\overline M\); in the certificate, nonvanishing of
\(M\) can instead be obtained from \(|\overline M C_0-1|<1\).

The leading center is

\[
C=1+\gamma\frac{M}{\overline M},
\]

and

\[
|C|\ge1-|\gamma|.
\]

Consequently, a sufficient final-time nonvanishing condition is

\[
|R_B|+|\gamma||R_A|
+|M||\gamma||C_\kappa-C_0|
+|M|E_t
<1-|\gamma|.
\]

This is strictly sharper than treating the whole A ladder as an error. The
constant term of the reflected ladder is retained in the center, exactly as
the bicomplex/idempotent interpretation requires.

The accompanying Arb pilot certifies that the current endpoint-cap bounds for
\(R_B\) and \(R_A\) do not close this inequality at the exact 0.14 row. That
is a negative result about this particular scalar majorant only. A successful
certificate still needs a phase-sensitive bound for the residual logarithmic
sums.
